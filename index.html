Clip Watcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. What Clip Watcher is

Clip Watcher is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It records public live streams, cuts short clips from them, and can upload those clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service. There is no Clip Watcher server, no user accounts, and no database. The only person who signs in to it is the person running it.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Clip Watcher uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Clip Watcher to upload, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

The Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles your data.

3. What Google account access it asks for

Clip Watcher asks for two permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
    Used to upload video files to the YouTube channel you choose when you sign in.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
    Used once, when you sign in, to read the name, handle and ID of the channel you chose. Clip Watcher shows you this so you can see where clips will go, and uses it to refuse to upload to a different channel.

It does not read your existing videos, comments, subscribers, viewing history, analytics, email address, contacts or any other Google account data.

You sign in on Google's own page in your web browser. Clip Watcher never sees or stores your Google password.

4. What it sends to YouTube

For each clip it uploads, Clip Watcher sends YouTube:

  • the video file,
  • a title,
  • a description, which credits and links the original streamer,
  • the privacy setting for the video (private, unlisted or public).

YouTube sends back the ID of the new video. Clip Watcher saves the video's link in a text file next to the clip on your computer, so the same clip is not uploaded twice.

5. What it stores, and where

Everything is stored on the computer that runs Clip Watcher. Nothing is sent to the operator or to any third party.

  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google, and the app's own client credentials, are kept in a folder in your home directory that only your user account on that computer can read. They are used only to upload videos when you have turned uploading on. The name, handle and ID of the channel you signed in to are kept in the same folder.
  • Recordings of the public live streams you chose to watch, and the clips cut from them.
  • The public chat of those streams (public usernames and messages), read without signing in. It is used to find the moments when chat reacts, and a short excerpt is saved next to each clip for your reference. Chat is not uploaded anywhere.

6. Sharing

Clip Watcher does not sell, rent, share or transfer your information to anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. The only data that leaves your computer is what is listed in section 4, and it goes only to YouTube, at your instruction.

Clip Watcher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information received from Google APIs is not used for advertising and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

7. Keeping, deleting and revoking

  • Sign-in tokens stay on your computer until you delete them or revoke access. Deleting the sign-in folder removes them.
  • You can revoke Clip Watcher's access to your Google account at any time on Google's security settings page. After that, the saved tokens stop working.
  • Videos uploaded to your channel are held by YouTube under your account. You can change or delete them in YouTube Studio.
  • Recordings, clips and chat logs stay on your computer until you delete them.

8. Children

Clip Watcher is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
